# Shark overview scientific illustrations

Four generated educational plates for Chapter 7- Sharks, Unit 1. They are not field photographs, a shared-scale size comparison, or a representation of one natural community.

## Generation and review

Mode: new image generation with the built-in image tool; no reference image input. Originals are preserved. Each selected PNG was converted without cropping to 1536 × 1024 WebP, quality 92.

Reviewed against the Florida Museum whale shark, scalloped hammerhead and shortfin mako profiles, NOAA Fisheries shortfin mako profile, Florida FWC hammerhead identification, and Monterey Bay Aquarium leopard shark profile linked in shared/sharks-content.mjs. Checked complete outlines, gill slits, fin arrangement, head shape, patterning, and tail. Perspective can obscure far-side paired fins. These simplified plates support comparison, not diagnostic identification on their own.
