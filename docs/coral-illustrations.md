# Coral illustration plates

Generated October 26, 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool (new-image mode). Six final educational specimen illustrations, full resolution 1536 × 1024. WebP copies use quality 92 without cropping; original PNGs remain in Codex generated-images storage. No existing illustration was overwritten.

Review focused on the diagnostic colony forms: flattened elkhorn fronds; cylindrical staghorn branches with corallites; a massive brain-coral dome with meandering ridges and valleys; rounded pillar columns with extended polyps; a connected reticulated sea fan; and a fleshy leather-coral cap with octocoral polyps. Anatomy and names were checked against the species and group references in `shared/coral-content.mjs`. These are generated teaching plates, not measured specimens or a substitute for taxonomic identification. The leather coral is deliberately labeled at genus level.

The initial sea-fan result had too few interconnected twigs. It was rejected and regenerated with a continuous fine closed mesh; only the revised version is used.
